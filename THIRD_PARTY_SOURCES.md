# Third-party sources and reuse notes

This repository contains original experiment code, model outputs, annotations,
and editorial figures, but it also refers to third-party models, artworks, and
published examples. Inclusion or citation here does not change the original
owner's license.

## Model and paper

- Beyer et al., [*PaliGemma 2: A Family of Versatile VLMs for
  Transfer*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03555).
- Model checkpoints:
  [`google/paligemma2-3b-mix-224`](https://huggingface.co/google/paligemma2-3b-mix-224)
  and
  [`google/paligemma2-3b-mix-448`](https://huggingface.co/google/paligemma2-3b-mix-448).
  Their access and use are governed by the terms shown on their model pages.

## Paintings

`data/paintings.csv` gives the official collection record and image-source page
for every painting in the experiment. The image files are intentionally excluded
from Git. Verify the source page's current terms before downloading,
redistributing, or publishing an image.

The blog figures additionally depict Claude Monet's *Impression, Sunrise*; the
source page used was
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Monet_-_Impression,_Sunrise.jpg).

## Published menu comparison

`reports/figures/menu_3b_vs_10b_comparison.png` annotates a menu image and model
outputs published in Hugging Face's
[“PaliGemma 2 Mix” article](https://huggingface.co/blog/paligemma2mix#text-recognition-in-images).
Those menu outputs were not generated by this experiment. The figure presents
short diagnostic excerpts and identifies substitutions or invented wording for
commentary and comparison.

## Generated figures

The architecture, SigLIP, Bronzino, and menu comparison layouts were created for
the accompanying blog. Embedded artworks or source photographs retain their
original rights status. Confirm reuse requirements before republishing the
figures outside a critical or educational context.
